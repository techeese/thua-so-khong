## 1. What it became — read as a stranger

A seven-person xóm on a riverbank, sixteen seasons, two hands a season. Each person is
`TÀI × GAN × BẠN`, hidden as `?` until your hand or the world touches a factor (v0.33). A factor at 1
is a literal zero — `chance()` = 0, momentum blocked (v0.30). The river is capital, the fourth
factor: sprouts scale ×0.37→×1.0 with it and it caps workshop tier (v0.31). The sky is law: storms
take a hand and stamp young roofs. You teach, hold a failure night, connect, put into the hụi or
take the pot, ask around about next season's sky, shelter a roof, build your own. People walk
errands weighted by their factors; a school compounds TÀI once Cô Mai blooms; an elder's clock
drains Chú Ba from season 8. Six endings, a 150-word card with the `×` arithmetic, a chronicle of
runs. Đông Hồ blocks, bilingual throughout, PWA, phone-fit.

**Load-bearing for the thesis** (systems the lab shows carry the multiplication): the cast with two
authored zeros · `chance()` with the hard floor · the hidden row and the quotes-as-clues · teach /
nerve / link · workshop tiers and the stamps that step them down · the river since v0.31 · the ending
arithmetic beat.

**Decoration or unproven** (survived because nobody removed them, or measured marginal): six year
cards · constraint runs · probe / shelter · hụi and pot as strategies (6.98/15.1 and 6.76/13.9 vs
hunter 6.98/16.1 — not spam channels, not decisive either) · the chronicle · the always-on tagline
· ambient chatter (4.5 bubbles/run, half of them blocked by the two-slot queue it shares with stat
floats) · the errand governor *as information* (37 % of known-person walks print under a label
already on screen; 63 % of errands carry no factor; **no low-TÀI walk exists**).

## 2. What the lab actually established (the arguments the next era must answer)

- **The band gate proves a race against a wasteful opponent, not that the zero decides.** 93 % of
  "diagnosis beats spreading" is the spreader wasting acts; a fair spreader is 6.96/15.5 vs hunter
  6.99/15.9. 68 % of the hunter's hands go to people with **no** low factor — the winning play is
  mostly polishing 4s and 5s (`decide.js`, `threefloors.js`).
- **Three thresholds wear one word.** Dice: ≤1. Label `zeroOf`: ≤3 (`×0` prefix only at 1,
  `gần không` at 2–3 since v0.32). Walks: ≤2. A person labelled "số không" blooms 4.5–13.5 % per
  season; the label was ~125–1000× more common than a true zero pre-v0.30 (`threefloors.js`,
  `zerowitness.js`, `walks.py`).
- **The thesis is told at t=0.** The sentence sits in the header subtitle and the intro tagline
  before a single hand is spent. Play itself tells rarely (3.5 % of strings; 1.2 tells per hunter
  run) — but the ending card is 150–158 words with 3–4 tells and a fixed great-man closing line, and
  it has listed ≤3-factor people as "bloomed" beside its own `×` arithmetic (`tell.py`).
- **Watching could be reading, and isn't yet.** An infinite observer names a GAN zero from walks
  82 % of the time, BẠN 65 %; a real watcher sees ~1.8–4.5 errands per run at play speed, and walks
  require `known`, so they never *precede* the label (`walks.py`).
- **Attachment decays.** Personal lines per villager 1.16 (s2) → 0.49 (s8) → 0.04 (s14). The
  literal zero the elder's clock creates is unwitnessed by despair, lift, or ending (`attach8.py`,
  `zerowitness.js`).
- **The price of being wrong is small.** Wrong factor −8 % tiers; wrong person costs nothing;
  labelOnly keeps 92 % of blooms (`decide.js`; LOOP.md re-measure: inverted 3.99/5.0 vs hunter
  6.98/16.1 — v0.30's floor paid most of it).

## 3. Unspent potential

The v0.33 hidden row plus v0.34 "the wrong place answers back" turned the game, for the first
time, into one where **information is the scarce thing** — you don't know the factor until you or
the world touches it. Nothing yet exploits that: walks still wait for `known`, no walk speaks for
TÀI, the ambient layer competes with stat floats for two slots, and the ending card still totals
what the play has already shown. The reductive findings (one threshold, drop the tagline, honest
card) are all cheap and all unspent. Run 2 has a chronicle but no memory of *what you learned*.

## 4. Candidate eras

### A · REDUCTIVE — "Một số không, một chữ" (one zero, one word)

**Intent:** make the game smaller and sharper: the word *số không* means exactly one thing
everywhere, and the game never says the thesis — the arithmetic does.

**Removes:** the header subtitle and intro tagline sentence (thesis lands from play, owner gate 3);
the ≤3 `zeroOf` label — a factor at 2–3 is *gần không* on the map, sheet, roster and card, and only
1 is `×0`; the ending card's "Đã nảy mầm" list of anyone whose min ≤3; the two-slot queue coupling
speech to stat floats (speech gets its own lane, rate unchanged); year cards that do not change
diagnosis (candidates: quiet-hands, restless-wind — measured first).
**Adds:** nothing new. Walk thresholds reconciled to the label (≤1 zero, 2–3 near) so the same
person reads the same on dice, sheet, road and card.
**Gates:** `threefloors` probe: one threshold — every surface (dice/label/walk/card) agrees on
every seed; `tell.py`: thesis-tells at t=0 = 0, ending card ≤ 90 words with `×` beat kept; card
lists no ≤3 as bloomed; ambient bubbles/run ≥ 8 with 0 blocked by floats; band holds ≥ current.
**Cost / might ruin:** the game gets quieter and less generous — the owner's "add something"
sessions pull the other way; removing the tagline risks a first-time player never getting the
sentence at all (that is the bet — and the owner gate is written exactly for it).

### B · REAL SWING — "Nhìn xóm mà đọc" (watching is reading)

**Intent:** change what *playing* is: diagnosis becomes observation. No factor label ever prints;
you learn a person by watching how they walk, hearing what they say, and by what your hand does
to them. The xóm is the instrument.

**Removes:** map labels and roster tags for factors; the `%` sprout hint on unstarted people;
`hỏi thăm` as a paid verb (the sky is read from the river and the birds, not bought).
**Adds:** a **TÀI walk** (there is none — a low-TÀI person carries the wrong tool, drops the load at
the market); walks that fire **before** `known` (the stranger reads first, names later); walk rate
tuned so a watcher sees ≥ 6 informative errands per run at play speed; a *watcher* band strategy
that acts only on what walks show, required to land within 15 % of the hunter.
**Gates:** watcher-band within 15 % of hunter and ≥ 2× idle; observer probe names GAN/BẠN/TÀI zeros
≥ 70 % each from walks alone; zero numeric factor text on canvas until touched; ambient still
silent and numberless (charter 4 — walks are motion, not numbers).
**Cost / might ruin:** the felt gate "diagnosis feels like play" could go either way — this is where
it lives or dies; a phone player who never watches loses the game's legibility; big rewrite of the
governor and the sheet; two owner loops currently *add* labels and hints, so this era would remove
their recent work.

### C · "Người làm chứng" (the witness) — attachment and the ending

**Intent:** every zero is witnessed by someone in the xóm, and the ending is one line per fate,
not a paragraph.

**Removes:** the 150-word card; the fixed great-man closing line; the ×2 dialogue budget for lines
that decay to 0.04/villager by season 14 (fewer lines, placed later).
**Adds:** the elder's-clock zero seen — someone speaks it, once, no number; a *fates* card:
seven one-liners with the `×` beat per person; a season-8 attachment gate (≥ 1 personal line per
villager between s6–s10); a "fates seen" chronicle hook (parked candidate).
**Gates:** ending card ≤ 7 lines + one `×` beat; every literal zero produced in a run has a
witness event; personal lines per villager at s8 ≥ 0.9; tells at ending ≤ 1.
**Cost / might ruin:** authored writing under a *less narration* directive; the owner has said "I
want to see what already exists before anyone adds to it".

### D · REDUCTIVE-SWING — "Ba bàn tay" (three hands)

**Intent:** the neighbour has three verbs and a river that fills itself. Remove hụi, pot, probe,
shelter, build. Capital rises only from blooms and the year clock; law is weather you cannot buy.

**Removes:** five verbs, the pot, the probe economy, the shelter, the player's own workshop (a
founder affordance in all but name — charter 1 pressure).
**Adds:** nothing; the river's flywheel is retuned so it still ×0.37→×1.0 without hụi.
**Gates:** band with three verbs ≥ 90 % of the current hunter score; the river still reaches 10 in
≥ 80 % of hunter runs by s12; no verb touches capital or law directly.
**Cost / might ruin:** the hụi is the one place the xóm *acts together* (charter: "contribute to
the hụi" is in the charter's own description of the neighbour) — removing it may cut the collective
thread the fable is about; the pot shipped this morning under owner authority.
