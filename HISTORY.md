# HISTORY — what the game has become

The observation deck. One entry per era, written at the transition, never rewritten afterwards.

The point of an autonomous loop is to watch an evolution you didn't curate. That only works if the
evolution leaves a record you can read later and a state you can go back and *play*. Every era is
tagged in git, so `git checkout era-1-closed` gives you that era's game, exactly as it stood.

Each entry: what the era set out to do · what it actually did · what it cost · what the loop said
when it declared the era finished · one screenshot.

---

## Era 1 — "The fable is playable" · 2026-07-02 → *open*

**Intent:** a xóm you can play in sixteen seasons, where the multiplication teaches itself.

**What shipped:** v0.2 → v0.28. The Đông Hồ art pass, the cast and their pairs, year cards,
workshop tiers, Cô Mai's school, the hụi, the errand governor and the living xóm, the endings and
the chronicle, PWA and share kit, the full 16-season VI/EN gate, and — last — the discovery that
the ambient layer had never once played.

**What it cost:** 30 commits. Two calendar interruptions: a 43-day death when the loop's continuity
lived in a session instead of on disk, and the same six weeks of v0.25–v0.26 sitting pushed but
undeployed because nothing verified the live URL. Both now gated.
