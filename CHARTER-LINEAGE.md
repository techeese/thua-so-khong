# CHARTER LINEAGE — every thesis this artifact has held

**Append-only. A line is never removed, reworded, or reordered.**

The loop may change the charter, but only at an **L4 · THESIS** transition, and only by appending
here first. `done.sh` verifies that the hash in `CHARTER.lock` appears in this file — so a charter
the loop rewrote without recording is a red gate, and every thesis the artifact has ever held stays
permanently readable.

This is what replaces the old absolute lock. The charter is no longer unreachable; it is
**auditable**. Drift is still possible in principle, but it can no longer be invisible: if thesis 4
is weaker than thesis 1, that is legible here in one screen.

## The ratchet applies to theses

A successor thesis must be **at least as demanding** as the one it replaces. "Mười nhân với không
vẫn bằng không" is a hard claim to make a game prove — a successor that is easier to satisfy is a
retreat wearing ambition's clothes, and the drift critic exists to reject it. Record the difficulty
argument in the entry, not just the change.
